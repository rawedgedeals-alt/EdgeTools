WHATIF / Website Builder Comparison

Webflow vs Wix: start with how much design control you actually need.

Both platforms can put a professional small-business website online without code. They start from different priorities: Webflow emphasizes visual site design and content management, while Wix is a general website builder that also offers commerce options. This guide compares the two workflows in plain terms so you can pick the one that fits the site you are actually building.

By WHATIF · Reviewed November 17, 2026

The quick answer

Consider Webflow if a visually distinctive site is the priority — a custom marketing site, portfolio or content-driven brand experience where detailed layout control matters more than getting online fast. Consider Wix if you want a general-purpose builder for a design-led website with services, bookings or informational content, and selling as one optional feature among several.

Method: based on the providers' public positioning and WHATIF's existing editorial guides, with our advice on choosing a workflow. We have not run a head-to-head benchmark or measured build times. Features, plan limits and prices can change — check the providers directly.

When Webflow makes sense

Webflow may fit better if the website itself is the product of the work: a brand presentation, a portfolio, or a content site where a generic template would undersell you. Its visual design controls are the draw. The trade-off WHATIF notes in its other guides is learning time — budget for it, especially if the person maintaining the site is not a designer.

When Wix makes sense

Wix may suit a business that prioritizes visual page design, services, bookings or informational content, with a smaller online store as one feature. If most visitors come to book, call or find you — rather than to check out — a general builder can be the simpler home, provided the plan you pick includes the features you need.

Compare total costs, not headline prices

For either platform, estimate the subscription after any introductory promotion, plus your domain, paid apps or add-ons, payment processing if you sell, and the time required to maintain the site. Prices and features change, so check the providers directly before committing. Our free software cost calculator can help you total the first year.

Questions that decide it

  • Is the site mainly a brand showcase, or a working tool for bookings and inquiries?
  • How much custom layout control do you need beyond a good template?
  • Will you sell — and if so, how much of the business depends on checkout?
  • Who will update the site, and how much learning time can they give it?
  • Can you export your content if you later change platforms?

Is Webflow or Wix better for a beginner?
This guide does not establish an overall winner. If fastest-to-a-finished-page matters most, a template-led builder is the simpler starting point. If the site's look is a competitive advantage and you can invest learning time, Webflow's design controls are the reason to choose it. Try the free or starter options with your real content before paying.

Can both sell online?
Yes, in different ways. Webflow offers e-commerce features; Wix offers selling on eligible commerce plans. If selling becomes the main job, also read our Shopify vs Webflow and Shopify vs Wix guides — a commerce-first platform may fit better than either.

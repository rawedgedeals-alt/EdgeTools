How sponsorship works

Straightforward terms. Clear separation.

Do you charge to mention a company?

No. WHATIF may include factual, independently chosen software listings and comparisons for readers. Sponsors pay for enhanced brand presentation, clearly labeled promotional placements and commissioned content—not control of editorial rankings.

Can you promise a number of visitors or customers?

No. WHATIF is a growing publication. We provide the agreed placement and report measurable activity honestly. We won't inflate traffic, invent testimonials or promise results.

Can we submit creative and a demo link?

Yes, subject to editorial and compliance approval. Paid placements are labeled “Advertisement” or “Sponsored Advertising Content”, and paid outbound links are marked as sponsored for search engines. The company's participation is never implied without approval.

How do we start?

Send the company name, public product link, the business problem you solve and which package interests you. We'll check fit and availability before an invoice or agreement.

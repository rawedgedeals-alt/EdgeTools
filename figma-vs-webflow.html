Figma vs Webflow: one designs the site, the other publishes it.

These two names come up together because website projects often touch both — but they do different jobs. Figma is where a website's screens are designed and connected into a clickable prototype. Webflow is where a website is built as a live, working site with visual design controls and a CMS. This guide explains where each one fits, so you can tell whether you need one, the other, or both.

By WHATIF · Reviewed November 17, 2026

The quick answer

Start with Figma if the open question is what the site should look like and how its screens connect — an interface you can click through and revise before anything is built. Start with Webflow if the design direction is settled and the open question is getting a real site online that you can update through a CMS. Many projects use Figma first and Webflow second; neither replaces the other.

When the design is still the question

If the task is to explain how someone books, buys or signs in, draw the screens and the transitions between them. Figma Design's prototyping tools let you connect designs into interactive flows, and you can ask a reviewer to find the next action without your explanation. A prototype is useful for discussing behavior; it does not replace checking the finished site's real forms, payments or data handling.

When the build is the question

If the pages are agreed and you need the site live, Webflow's visual layout control and CMS are the relevant tools. It suits a visually distinctive marketing site, portfolio or content-driven brand experience. Its design workflow can take time to learn — that learning curve is the main cost to plan for.

A useful way to decide

  1. Name the deliverable. A design to approve, or a site to launch?
  2. Test the flow in Figma if people disagree about the design — changing a prototype is cheaper than rebuilding a page.
  3. Build in Webflow once the flow is settled, and check the real forms and checkout on the live site, not just the design.
  4. Agree the handoff. If a developer or a Webflow builder takes over, decide in advance which files, assets and specifications change hands.

Can Webflow replace Figma?
Only if your design process is simple enough to happen directly in the builder. Webflow builds real pages; it is not a free-form prototyping canvas. If you need to explore several directions or test a flow with reviewers first, that exploration is Figma's job.

Can Figma publish a website?
A Figma prototype is a design, not a hosted website. Turning it into a live site means building it — in Webflow, another builder, or with a developer. See also our Canva vs Figma guide if your design work is mostly everyday business content rather than interfaces.

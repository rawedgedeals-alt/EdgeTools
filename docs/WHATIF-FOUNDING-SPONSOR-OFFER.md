## Core positioning

WHATIF helps founders, small businesses and creators answer high-intent "What if I could...?" questions by comparing **real AI, automation, sales/marketing and commerce software**. Sponsor solutions to a practical business problem, **not purchased editorial reviews**.

Unlike RawEdge Casino Reviews, WHATIF is a software-discovery and business-tool publication. Keep the two brands, Git repositories, analytics streams, and ad inventories fully separate.

## Product / niche: small-business software that pays for itself

Target narrowly defined users with a purchase problem, not just browsers collecting tools:
1. **Make sales:** Store builders, CRM, appointment scheduling, email marketing (e.g. Shopify, HubSpot, Kit).
2. **Get discovered:** Search marketing, SEO and content workflows (e.g. Semrush, Buffer).
3. **Get time back:** Automate repetitive tasks with Make, Zapier or appropriate AI tools.

Use original guides, comparisons and the existing subscription-cost calculator to earn reader attention. Showcase a paid partner **only** in a prominently disclosed separate advertising area. The 20 existing factual listings can remain useful editorial discovery; no vendor owes us a fee merely for a factual mention.

## Homepage path and outreach

**User path**: problem-focused guide or calculator → relevant tools → optional separate sponsored solution → measured link → official software site.

**Vendor pitch**: “What if buyers looking to solve the exact problem your software fixes encountered your product in a professionally presented, clearly disclosed partner showcase? WHATIF is offering limited 90-day and 6-month founding placements with a custom use-case option, honest click reports, and introductory pricing.”

Focus initial cold outreach on growth-stage software makers with an approachable partnerships/growth manager, not only very large enterprises with high procurement hurdles. Begin with an optional 90-day pilot and offer the 6-month use-case package as the preferred product. Do not claim companies are customers or partners until signed.

## Editorial, consumer and SEO safeguards

- Label paid cards and sponsored use-case pages clearly near their headline / CTA, per FTC native advertising guidance.
- Mark paid and affiliate outbound links `rel="sponsored"` (plus `noopener noreferrer` for new tabs), per Google Search Central.
- Maintain accurate source-based comparisons; sponsorship cannot buy favorable rankings or fake first-hand product testing.
- Don't claim a company endorsed WHATIF or approved use of protected brand assets until it actually did.
- Keep advertising campaign flags off by default. Do not activate a sponsor before written agreement, approved creative, destination checks and invoices/payment requirements are met.
- No artificial clicks, fake view counts or guaranteed return on ad spend.
- No invoices, subscriptions, or credit-card collection exist automatically because a pricing page exists.

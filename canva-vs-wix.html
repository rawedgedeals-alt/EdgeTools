Canva vs Wix: start with the page you need to publish.

People compare these two because both can put something that looks like a website in front of customers. Underneath, they are different tools: Canva is a visual content platform — social posts, presentations, documents, video — that also offers simple one-page website publishing, while Wix is a dedicated website builder for multi-page sites with services, bookings and optional selling. This guide compares the jobs, not the logos.

By WHATIF · Reviewed November 17, 2026

The quick answer

Consider Canva if the job is one simple page — an event, a launch, a link-in-bio style page — and you already make your business visuals there. Consider Wix if the job is a real website: several pages, bookings or services, room to grow, and selling as an option. A one-page site is a starting point, not a substitute for a website your business will run on.

For one announcement page

Start with the thing due this week. If it is a single page for an event, a menu, or a campaign — and the same design will be reused as a flyer or social post — Canva's workflow keeps everything in one place. Judge the finished result at the size people will see it: is the offer legible on a phone, and can a customer find the next step?

For the website your business runs on

If customers will book appointments, browse services, read several pages or buy from you, that is a website builder's job. Wix is built for design-led websites where selling is one feature among several. Also read our Webflow vs Wix guide if detailed design control matters, and Shopify vs Wix if selling becomes the main job.

Try a real brief before subscribing

  1. Name the deliverable. One announcement page, or a site with pages for services, about and contact?
  2. Use the same content. Keep the words, imagery and audience consistent in both.
  3. Make one meaningful revision. Change the main offer and see how each tool handles it.
  4. Ask someone else to use it. Can they find the phone number, the booking step, or the buy button?
  5. Check the exit. If the page outgrows the tool, what moves with you and what gets rebuilt?

Can Canva replace a website builder?
For a single simple page, it can be enough. For a growing business site — multiple pages, bookings, a store, a blog — a dedicated builder is the appropriate tool. Starting on Canva and moving later is reasonable; just know the move is a rebuild, not a transfer.

Is Canva or Wix better for beginners?
This guide does not establish an overall winner. If you already design in Canva, its one-page publishing is the familiar starting point. If you have never made a website and need the whole thing, Wix's builder is designed for exactly that job. Try the free options with your real content first.

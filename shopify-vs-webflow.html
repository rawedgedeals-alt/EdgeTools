Shopify vs Webflow: which one fits your next website?

Both platforms can help you get a professional website online, but they start from different priorities. Shopify focuses on selling products. Webflow emphasizes visual site design and content management, with commerce options available.

Quick answer

Choose Shopify when managing an online store is your primary goal. Consider Webflow when visual design, brand presentation, and a content-focused website are your main priorities.

When Shopify makes sense

Shopify is worth exploring if you need a product catalog, checkout, order management, and an ecosystem of store-focused integrations. It's designed around commerce workflows rather than treating selling as an extra feature.

When Webflow makes sense

Webflow may fit better if you're creating a visually distinctive marketing site, portfolio, or content-driven brand experience. It provides extensive visual layout control, though its design workflow can take time to learn.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Will most visitors come to buy products, or to read about your services?
  • How important are inventory, checkout, shipping, and order management?
  • Do you prefer ready-made store themes or detailed visual design control?
  • What are the current subscription, transaction, and add-on costs for your use case?

Features, prices, and eligibility can change. Compare the latest details directly with each provider before signing up.

Shopify vs Wix: which should you use for an online store?

Both platforms can support online selling, but the better choice depends on how much of your business revolves around commerce versus content and design.

Choose Shopify when selling is the main job

Consider Shopify if you expect to manage a growing catalog, process orders regularly, track inventory or add specialized commerce integrations. Check the capabilities of the specific plan you intend to buy.

Choose Wix when the website comes first

Wix may suit a business that prioritizes visual page design, services, bookings or informational content, with a smaller online store as one feature. Verify which Wix plans include payment acceptance in your location.

Compare total costs, not promotional prices

Before committing, estimate the subscription after any introductory promotion, payment fees, paid apps, shipping and fulfillment, design costs and the time required to maintain the site. Prices and features change, so check the providers directly.

Questions that decide it

  • How many products will you sell in the first year?
  • Do you need local pickup, shipping or recurring purchases?
  • Is a blog, portfolio or appointment booking more important than checkout?
  • Which payment methods and countries must you support?
  • Can you export your data if you later change platforms?
